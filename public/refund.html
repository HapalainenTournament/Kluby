Refund policy

Clubroom Pass is sold through Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record, which processes all payments and refunds.

14-day refund

If you are not happy with Clubroom Pass, you can get a full refund within 14 days of any payment – the first one or a monthly renewal. No reason needed.

How to ask for a refund

Reply to your Paddle receipt email, or find your order at paddle.net using the email you paid with and request a refund there. You can also write to us at [DOPLNIT support email] and we will arrange it. Refunds go back to the original payment method; how long the money takes to arrive depends on your bank.

What happens to the Pass

A refunded payment ends the Pass for that club. The club's archive is kept for 30 days in case you come back; after that only the free limit may be kept.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops future payments. The Pass stays active until the end of the month you already paid for. Use the link in your Paddle receipt email or paddle.net.

This policy does not limit any statutory rights you have as a consumer.
