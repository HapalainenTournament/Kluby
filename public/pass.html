Who pays?

One person – usually the captain – pays for the club. Everyone who opens the club in Clubroom gets the Pass benefits.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. The Pass stays active until the end of the paid period, then the club returns to the free plan.

What happens to our archive when the Pass ends?

Your full archive is kept for 30 days after the Pass ends. After that the club returns to the free plan, which keeps the newest 10 matches of each match type (league, playoff…) – the same window EA shows.
