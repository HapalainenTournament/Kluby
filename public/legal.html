Clubroom Pass

Clubroom Pass is an optional subscription for one club: 4 € per month (the final price including tax is shown at checkout). It renews automatically every month until cancelled. While it is active, everyone who opens that club gets the Pass benefits: the full match archive is kept beyond the free limit of the newest 10 matches per match type, Misc insights are unlocked with fewer ads, shareable cards have no watermark and the club gets the gold Pass look. No member accounts are needed. To buy a Pass you must be 18 or have your parent's or guardian's permission.

Who you buy from. Payments are processed by Stripe and Clubroom Pass is sold through Link, Stripe's merchant of record service. Link appears on your receipt and bank statement (LINK.COM*CLUBROOM), handles taxes, receipts and payment support, and its purchase terms apply to the payment. Clubroom provides the service itself.

Cancelling. Cancel any time at app.link.com with the email you paid with, or write to us. The Pass stays active until the end of the month you already paid for. After it ends, the club's extra archive is kept for at least 30 days; after that only the free limit may be kept. Price changes are announced in advance and never apply to a period you already paid for.

Refunds

Not happy? You get a full refund within 14 days of any payment – the first one or a renewal, no reason needed. Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the email you paid with, or contact Link support. Refunds go back to the original payment method. A refunded payment ends the Pass for that club; the archive is kept for 30 days in case you come back. This does not limit any statutory consumer rights.

Cookies & ads

Today Clubroom uses no tracking cookies. It stores a few things only in your own browser (local storage) because you asked for them: favourite and recent clubs, open tabs and filters, revealed awards and similar preferences. They never leave your device and need no consent; clear them any time in your browser settings.

When ads are switched on, Clubroom will show ads from Google AdSense. Before any advertising cookies or identifiers are used, you'll see a consent message from a Google-certified consent platform (IAB TCF). You can accept, refuse or choose purposes; refusing still lets you use Clubroom with limited, non-personalised ads. You can change your choice any time via Privacy choices at the bottom of the page. More: how Google uses data from partner sites. We block ad categories that don't belong on a football stats site, such as gambling and dating.
